Quick help

Try these steps before contacting support.

Purchase not showing

  1. Confirm that you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  2. Restart the game and open the in-game store again.
  3. Use Restore Purchases for eligible non-consumable purchases.

Cloud save or load problem

  1. Confirm that iCloud Drive and Game Center are enabled on the device.
  2. Check your internet connection and available iCloud storage.
  3. Restart the game before trying Save or Load again.

Ads are unavailable

Check your connection, disable network-level content blocking temporarily and try again later. Ad availability can vary by location and time.

Crash or low performance

Restart the device, close unused apps and install the latest game and iOS updates. Lower the graphics quality if the device becomes hot or the frame rate drops.

Frequently asked questions

Can I move an Android cloud save to iPhone?

Android and iOS currently use separate cloud-save services. A Google Play Games save does not automatically transfer to iCloud.

Can consumable purchases be restored?

Consumable items generally cannot be restored through Apple’s Restore Purchases feature. Contact support if a completed purchase was not delivered.

Why are personalized ads not appearing?

Personalized advertising depends on your consent choices, age, region and ad availability. The game may show non-personalized ads or no ad when an eligible ad is unavailable.

What to include in a report

Please do not send your Apple Account password, payment-card information or other sensitive information.

  • Device model and iOS version
  • Game version
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • Steps that reproduce the problem
  • A screenshot or short screen recording, if useful
