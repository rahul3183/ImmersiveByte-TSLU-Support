We do not intentionally request government identification numbers, contacts, SMS messages, call logs, health information, microphone recordings, precise GPS location or full payment-card details. The Game does not currently require a separate ImmersiveBytes account.

5. Advertising and tracking choices

The Game may show personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your region, age, consent choices, device settings and applicable law. Advertising providers may process advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device information, product interactions, ads viewed, diagnostic information and performance data.

The Game asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing the advertising identifier for tracking. You can change this choice in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where required, the Game also presents Google’s privacy and consent options.

Rejecting tracking does not remove every advertisement. The Game may continue to display contextual or non-personalized ads, and providers may process limited information for security, fraud prevention, basic measurement and service operation where permitted.

6. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional purchases such as virtual currency, bundles and other digital content. Apple processes payments through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment-card information. We may receive transaction status, product, price and currency information needed to deliver items, analyze purchases, provide support and prevent fraud.

7. Cloud saves and platform services

If you use cloud save, the Game sends save data to iCloud and uses Game Center for platform features. Apple controls the associated platform account and processes its player identifier and cloud data under its own privacy terms.

8. Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child without consent required by applicable law. Children should use the Game with permission and supervision from a parent or legal guardian where required.

If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information for money. We may disclose information:

  • To the service providers described above so they can operate their services.
  • To advertising partners for ad delivery, personalization and measurement where permitted. Some laws may treat this activity as “sharing” or “selling” for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process or a valid government request.
  • To protect players, our rights, the Game, service providers or the public from harm, fraud or misuse.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, financing or transfer of the Game or business, subject to applicable law.

We and our service providers retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Game, analyze performance, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Retention periods vary by data type and provider. Support correspondence may be kept while a request is active and as needed for business or legal records.

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your choices and requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing. You can also:

  • Change tracking and advertising choices in device settings and available in-game privacy options.
  • Change notification permission in device settings.
  • Manage iCloud, Game Center and purchase history through your Apple Account.
  • Remove locally stored game data by deleting the app, subject to cloud backups that remain in your platform account.

Email requests to immersivebyte@gmail.com. Include the Game name, any available player or device identifier and a clear description of the request. Do not send your Apple Account password, full payment-card information or other unnecessary sensitive information.
